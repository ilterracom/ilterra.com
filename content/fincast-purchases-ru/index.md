This article provides detailed information about the paid and free functionality of the Fincast application.

The key feature of the application (for all platforms and versions) is the absence of ads.

### Basic (free functionality)

| Platform | Version | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iOS (iPhone, iPad) | >= 1.0 | – Displaying the current exchange rate for 4 currencies from the basket of the National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (US dollar, Euro, Chinese yuan, Russian ruble) – Displaying a chart of exchange rate changes for each position of the currency basket |
| Apple Watch | >= 1.0 | Displaying the current exchange rate for 4 currencies from the basket of the National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (US dollar, Euro, Chinese yuan, Russian ruble) indicating the date of setting this rate value |

### “Advanced functionality”

**“Extended functionality”** – a one-time purchase (pay once and use without taking into account the time) that provides access to additional offline functionality of the application. The acquired “advanced functionality” remains available to the buyer in all subsequent versions. The composition of the extended functionality package can be supplemented with new useful functions that will become available in future releases of the application, and, accordingly, the composition of the existing functions will be determined by the actual functionality of the current version of the application.

| **Platform** | **Version** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iOS (iPhone, iPad) | >= 1.1 | – the ability to sort the display order of currency rate widgets (using the “up arrow” and “down arrow” keys – functionality of the built-in currency converter |
| iOS (iPhone, iPad) | *>= 1.2 (планируется)* | – *access to detailed analytical information for each exchange rate* |
| Apple Watch | *планируется* |  |

The “Advanced Functionality” package does not include access to the product’s online service, namely, services distributed in the form of subscriptions and providing access to analytical data produced by FinCast team specialists (forecast indicators and other online services).
