## Vision

**[o0o.app](https://o0o.app)** transforms Telegram into a complete short-link and QR automation workspace.  
It merges instant, conversational link creation with a secure, data-driven dashboard — giving teams a single place to manage redirects, campaigns and visitor insights.

---

## Why Teams Choose o0o.app

**🚀 Instant creation — right inside Telegram**  
Share any URL with [@urlcompressor\_bot](https://t.me/urlcompressor_bot) and instantly receive a branded short link and QR asset.  
No switching between apps — mobile teams work directly from Telegram.

**🎯 Adaptive destination control**  
Each QR or short link can route visitors to multiple targets with configurable probabilities.  
Ideal for A/B testing, localisation or load balancing without reprinting physical materials.

**📊 Analytics that drive action**  
The web dashboard visualises engagement, tracks trends, and lets operators search, filter and export link-level performance data.

**🔐 Verified and secure access**  
Operators sign in with multi-factor authentication and link their Telegram identity through a verified two-step flow — ensuring only trusted users manage live campaigns.

**🏢 Enterprise-ready foundation**  
Every redirect, session and ownership record is logged with timestamps and client signals, supporting compliance today and data intelligence tomorrow.

---

## How It Works

1. **Authenticate securely.**  
   Log in once with email, password and one-time code. Optionally link your Telegram ID for one-tap access in the field.
2. **Generate links conversationally.**  
   Send any destination to the bot to receive a short URL and QR image — ready for print, signage or digital sharing.
3. **Optimise in the dashboard.**  
   Track scans, adjust routing probabilities, manage campaigns and download assets without changing existing materials.

## Business Impact

**Higher conversion from offline media**  
One QR can adaptively redirect each visitor to the most relevant landing page — by language, promotion or stock level — improving conversions without extra prints.

**Faster campaign launch**  
Telegram-native workflows let distributed teams create and adjust campaigns instantly, ideal for time-sensitive marketing.

**Unified intelligence**  
Operators see exactly which assets perform, when peaks occur, and how experiments affect behaviour — enabling confident marketing and product decisions.

## Security & Compliance

- Mandatory multi-factor authentication and encrypted sessions protect operator accounts.
- Telegram linking requires verified TOTP codes before access to sensitive data.
- All redirect and token events are logged in auditable MySQL tables, providing a clear trail for compliance and enterprise integration.
