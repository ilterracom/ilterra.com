## What is MIDI Converter?

**MIDI Converter** is a web-based tool designed for musicians, producers, and composers who work with MIDI files in professional digital audio workstations such as **Logic Pro**, Ableton Live, Cubase, FL Studio, and others.

The application solves a common but frustrating problem:

many MIDI files contain inconsistent tempo information across different notes and tracks, which can cause incorrect playback, timing issues, and broken arrangements when importing into DAWs — especially Logic Pro.

MIDI Converter automatically transforms such MIDI files into a clean, standardized format with a **single unified tempo for the entire composition**, ensuring that notes are placed correctly and durations are preserved exactly as intended.

## The Problem

Many MIDI packs and third-party MIDI generators export files where:

- Tempo may change randomly between notes
- Different tracks may contain conflicting tempo data
- Timing is encoded in a way that some DAWs interpret incorrectly
- Logic Pro may display wrong BPM or stretch notes improperly

As a result:

- MIDI files sound out of sync
- Notes appear shifted or compressed
- Musical timing becomes unreliable
- Manual fixing takes a lot of time

This is especially common with MIDI files generated by algorithmic tools, old sequencers, or certain notation software.

## The Solution

MIDI Converter analyzes the timing structure of your MIDI file and rebuilds it into a clean, DAW-friendly format where:

- The entire composition uses a **single global tempo**
- Note positions are recalculated correctly
- Note durations are preserved
- Timing becomes deterministic and predictable
- The file imports perfectly into Logic Pro and other DAWs

The result is a professional-grade MIDI file that behaves exactly as expected in your project.
